# Clinical Safety & Healthcare Network Infrastructure Resilience

## Healthcare & Clinical Device Safety

In hospital and healthcare cyber-physical systems (IoMT - Internet of Medical Things), network availability directly impacts patient life safety:

- **Infusion Pumps & Patient Monitors**: Connected telemetry devices rely on uninterrupted network access for vital alarms.
- **DICOM & PACS Medical Imaging**: High-volume transmission of medical scans must not be throttled as a false-positive volumetric flood.
- **Fail-Safe Operation**: In healthcare operational environments, DDoS mitigation algorithms must never disconnect life-support or real-time clinical monitoring endpoints. Any automated throttling must prioritize IoMT QoS classes.
