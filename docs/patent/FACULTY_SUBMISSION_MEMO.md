1. **80% Government Fee Concession**:

2. **Form 18A Expedited Fast-Track Examination**:
   - Under Rule 24C of the Patents Rules, educational institutions are eligible for **Expedited Examination**. This reduces the patent grant timeline from the typical 4–5 years down to **12–18 months**!
3. **Immediate "Patent Pending" Status**:
   - The moment the online e-filing is completed at the Delhi Patent Office, an official Patent Application Number (e.g., `2026110XXXXX`) is generated immediately.
4. **Freedom to Publish**:
   - Once the Application Number is secured, our team can immediately submit our papers to IEEE/Elsevier conferences or journals and publish on GitHub without any risk of losing patent rights.
