## 📥 Method 1: Instant 1-Click PDF Download (No LaTeX Required)

You do **not** need to install LaTeX on your computer. You can download the camera-ready double-column PDF right now from your Mac:

1. Open the formatted paper in your browser:
   ```bash
   open docs/paper/IEEE_TRANSACTIONS_RESEARCH_PAPER.html
   ```
2. Your browser will open the paper formatted in **IEEE Two-Column Journal Style** (with Times New Roman font, KaTeX mathematical formulas, and scientific tables).
3. Click the cyan **"📥 Download / Print as PDF"** button in the top bar (or press `Cmd + P` on your keyboard).
4. In the print dialog:
   - **Destination**: Choose **"Save as PDF"**.
   - **Paper Size**: Letter or A4.
   - **Margins**: None / Default.
   - **Options**:
     - ✅ **CHECK *"Background graphics"*** (to render table borders and highlights).
     - ❌ **UNCHECK *"Headers and footers"*** (🚨 **CRITICAL**: This completely removes the `.html` file path, user path, and date from the top and bottom of your PDF!).
5. Click **Save**! You now have a clean, pristine IEEE Transactions PDF on your desktop without any browser links.

## 🌐 Method 2: Compile on Overleaf (For Formal IEEE Submissions)

[Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/) is the world’s leading online LaTeX collaborative editor used by IEEE and Elsevier:

1. Go to [https://www.overleaf.com/](https://www.overleaf.com/) and log in (free).
2. Click **New Project** ➔ **Blank Project** (name it `DDoS-AI-IEEE-Paper`).
3. Delete the default files in the left sidebar.
4. Click the **Upload** icon and upload:
   - [`IEEE_TRANSACTIONS_RESEARCH_PAPER.tex`](IEEE_TRANSACTIONS_RESEARCH_PAPER.tex)
   - [`references.bib`](references.bib)
5. In the left panel, click on `IEEE_TRANSACTIONS_RESEARCH_PAPER.tex` and click the green button: **Recompile**.
6. Overleaf will generate the official IEEE PDF on the right side. Click **Download PDF**.

## 🔬 Method 3: Uploading to ResearchGate

Uploading your paper to **ResearchGate** establishes your public scholarly profile and allows professors and researchers worldwide to read and cite your work:

1. Go to [https://www.researchgate.net/](https://www.researchgate.net/) and log in with your IUST student/faculty email (`@iust.ac.in`).
2. Click the green button: **"Add new research"** (top right).
3. Select **"Preprint"** (or **"Article"**):
   - Choose *"Upload a file"* and select your generated `IEEE_TRANSACTIONS_RESEARCH_PAPER.pdf`.
4. Fill in the publication details:
   - **Title**: `Cascading Dual-Tier Architecture for Line-Rate DDoS Prevention and Autonomous Mitigation in Software-Defined Networks`
   - **Authors**:
     - Dar Rehan Rasool (Lead Author)
     - Muneeb Sayed (Co-Author)
     - Shafiya Bashir Pampori (Co-Author)
     - Dr. Javad Ahmad (Senior Author & Supervisor)
   - **Date**: October 2026
   - **DOI**: Paste your Zenodo DOI (`10.5281/zenodo.14892345`).
5. Copy and paste the Abstract from `IEEE_TRANSACTIONS_RESEARCH_PAPER.md`.
6. Click **Publish**! Your research paper is now indexed globally on ResearchGate and Google Scholar.
