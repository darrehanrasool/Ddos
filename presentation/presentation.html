The 3 Attack Types

The 3 Ways Attackers Try to Ruin the Restaurant

Every DDoS attack in the world falls into one of these three pranks.

1. The Bulldozer Mob
• The Prank: 3,000 screaming people try to shove through your door at once.
• Result: Street and door are physically jammed!
• In Computers: Volumetric Floods (UDP / DNS Reflection). Chokes internet pipes with gigabits of trash.
2. The Frozen Handshake
• The Prank: A guy reaches out to shake hands with the cashier: "Hi, can I order?" When the cashier reaches out, the guy freezes and stares blankly without ordering. Soon, the cashier's hands are all tied up!
• In Computers: TCP SYN Flood (Neptune). Sends SYN=1, but never completes the ACK handshake.
3. The Sly Kahwa Sipper
• The Prank: 10 guys in suits walk in, take all 10 tables, order 1 cup of Kahwa, and take one tiny sip every 45 minutes for 8 hours.
• Result: Tables are 100% full, but bandwidth is zero!
• In Computers: Slowloris. Holds web server threads hostage with low bandwidth.
Why Previous Work Failed

The 4 Big Mistakes Other Researchers Made

Why traditional academic models fail when tested in the real world.

Mistake 1: The "Suit-Only" Silo
Researchers trained their AI only to recognize office workers in suits. When tested on IoT smart lightbulbs or SDN switches, the AI panicked and collapsed!
Mistake 2: The "Lazy Detective" Lag
Researchers used heavy recurrent models (BiLSTM) that take 30 milliseconds per packet. At 10 Gbps flood speeds, a line of 10,000 packets forms, overflowing the switch!
Mistake 3: The "Watcher, Not Doer"
Over 95% of published papers only print: "Hey, look, an attack happened!" but do NOTHING to stop it. The server still crashes.
Mistake 4: Crashing the Laptop
Their code requires supercomputers and crashes with out-of-memory errors on normal laptops due to unbatched memory leaks.

Detection Clues

How Does the AI Know It's Fake?

The 3 dead giveaways that reveal an attack botnet.

1. Shannon Entropy
• Normal Traffic: High variety (tea, kebabs, rice, juice). High Entropy = Healthy!
• Attack Traffic: 10,000 bots order the exact same 64-byte tap water. Variety collapses to zero = Low Entropy!
2. Flow Symmetry
• Normal Traffic: Two-way conversation (You ask Google for a picture, Google sends it back).
• Attack Traffic: Attacker shouts 10,000 packets forward and receives 0 replies (100% one-way!).
3. TCP Handshake Flags
• Normal Traffic: Handshake finished cleanly (SYN then ACK).
• Attack Traffic: Attackers spam SYN=1 and leave their hands frozen in mid-air (ACK=0).
