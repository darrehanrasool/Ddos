If you want to retrain or experiment (for example, with more training epochs or on your own custom CSV):

bash
# Retrain all 6 datasets for 10 epochs using Apple Silicon GPU:
.venv/bin/python src/ddos_ai/main.py train-datasets --dataset all --epochs 10

# Or retrain only one specific dataset (e.g. SDN-DDoS):
.venv/bin/python src/ddos_ai/main.py train-datasets --dataset sdn_ddos --epochs 10
